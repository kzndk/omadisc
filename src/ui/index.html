A place for every conversation.

Open Settings and sign in to Discord once. All panes share your saved sign-in and reconnect automatically. Choose 1, 2, 4 or 6 panes above, then use each pane’s + button to add a channel.

Use All channels in the toolbar to jump to any saved channel, including one hidden by the current layout. Back to grid restores your layout. Channel assignments persist after quitting.

Chat views use your desktop palette, compact messages and monospace typography. Use ☰ in a pane header to browse servers, channels and members; use ← to return to chat. Selecting another channel also returns to chat. Turn off Use Omarchy chat design in Settings for the original Discord view.

Independent browser workspace. Not affiliated with Discord. No bots or extracted tokens. Discord supplies the messages and editor beneath the Omarchy design. Voice, video, screen sharing and desktop notifications are not enabled in this text-first release.
